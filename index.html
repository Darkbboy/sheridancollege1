Help Desk Operations Plan Overview

Hello, I am the Help Desk Supervisor.

Reason for the Plan:

This plan outlines our Help Desk goals for the next fiscal year.

Mission and Visions:

Mission:

Provide fast technical support maximizing user productivity.

Vision:

The Help Desk will be a world-class technology resource center.

Smart goal and benefits

Five SMART Goals

GOAL 1

- Decrease call abandonment rate fifteen percent by quarter four.

- Users receive faster service.

GOAL 2

- Increase First Call Resolution ninety percent by December.

- Technical issues are fixed immediately.

GOAL 3

- Improve Customer Satisfaction Score ninety-five percent by June.

- Better support experiences for all users.

GOAL 4

- Reduce Average Handle Time eight minutes by fiscal year end.

- More staff time available for critical work.

GOAL 5

- Increase staff training completion one hundred percent by March.

- Staff are highly skilled for support needs.
