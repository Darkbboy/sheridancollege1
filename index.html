Help Desk Operations Plan

Hello, I am the Help Desk Supervisor. This plan outlines our goals for the next fiscal year.

Mission

Provide fast technical support maximizing user productivity.

Vision

The Help Desk will be a world-class technology resource center.

Five SMART Goals

GOAL 1: Call Abandonment

Decrease rate fifteen percent by quarter four.

Benefit:

Users receive faster service.

GOAL 2: First Call Resolution (FCR)

Increase to ninety percent by December.

Benefit:

Technical issues are fixed immediately.

GOAL 3: Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)

Improve score to ninety-five percent by June.

Benefit:

Better support experiences for all users.

GOAL 4: Average Handle Time (AHT)

Reduce by eight minutes by fiscal year end.

Benefit:

More staff time available for critical work.

GOAL 5: Staff Training

Increase completion to one hundred percent by March.

Benefit:

Staff are highly skilled for support needs.
